Common Path

The selector mirror, just after the Coudé focal plane, also works as pupil stabiliser. OAP1 images the pupil on the DM, with a fast tip-tilt mirror upstream to offload tip-tilt from the DM. OAP2, identical to OAP1, forms an intermediate focal plane. FM5 and FM6 fold the beam so everything from OAP1 to the dichroic fits on one breadboard.

Components
Pupil stabiliser30 mm after the F/20.5 focus. 1° tilt: PSF shift 1.05 mm (5.8″ on sky), pupil shift 73% of diameter. Max needed ≈ 0.33° for 25% pupil drift (FoV drift ≈ 1.9″).
Fast tip-tilt130 mm upstream of the DM. 1″ on sky = 0.008° rotation = 0.11% pupil shift on the DM.
DM468Pupil 31.6 mm, 12° AOI → 31.6 × 32.3 mm on a 33 mm clear aperture. Measured flat ≈ 6 nm RMS, TT stroke ≈ 15 µm surface.
DichroicLong-pass, AOI 15°. Custom part with 0.1° wedge on the second face to reduce astigmatism and NCPA; quotations pending.
StatusAligned in Padova (June 2026), transmitted WFE ≈ 55 nm RMS over 33 mm.

WFS Channel

BS1 sends about 30% of the visible light to the VIS port. A re-imaging lens puts the pupil on the modulator and makes an F/45 focus. BS2 sends about 10% to the Gain Scheduling Camera (GSC) and 90% to the pyramid. A fold mirror before the pyramid focus can feed visiting WFSs.

Components
BS1Thorlabs BSS10 (30:70) or BSN10 (10:90), UV fused silica, 30′ wedge, used at 20° AOI
BS2Same options as BS1, 10:90 to the GSC
Modulator (PI-E727)Pupil 7.3 mm (unprojected), AOI 14° (28° deflection). 1 λ/D at 800 nm = 0.003° on one axis, 0.003°/cos 14° on the other.
Double pyramidArcetri, two glued N-BK7 pyramids, face angle 3.05°, F/45. Cone angle 0.64°, deviation 1.577°. Average RMS WFE at tip 56 nm; 90% EE within ≈ 93 µm.
Pupil re-imagerAC254-045-B: 42 px pupils, 2.49 mm (104 px) separation. (AC254-060-B would give 55.5 px and 3.32 mm / 138 px.)
WFS cameraOCAM2K, 24 µm pixels
GSC cameraC-Blue, F/45, 30″ FoV, 2.52″/mm, exit pupil 330 mm before focus. 18.4 × 13.9″ field, 23 mas/px, λ/D at 600 nm = 68 mas (3 px).
Removing a beam splitter
BS1 outDefocus 123 nm RMS (focus shift 6.9 mm at F/45), astigmatism 19 nm RMS. 4.4 mrad deviation: PSF shift ≈ 700 mas, pupil shift ≈ 10%.
BS2 outDefocus 21 nm RMS (focus shift 1.2 mm at pyramid), astigmatism 10 nm RMS. PSF shift ≈ 380 mas, pupil shift ≈ 18%.

Source: Design Description Document (EKARUS VIS). Values from the nominal design, without manufacturing tolerances.

The VIS science port receives about 30% of the visible light reflected by BS1. It initially hosts a visible camera.
