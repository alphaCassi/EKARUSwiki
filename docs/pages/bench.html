Common Path

The selector mirror, just after the Coudé focal plane, also works as pupil stabiliser. OAP1 images the pupil on the DM, with a fast tip-tilt mirror upstream to offload tip-tilt from the DM. OAP2, identical to OAP1, forms an intermediate focal plane. FM5 and FM6 fold the beam so everything from OAP1 to the dichroic fits on one breadboard.

Components
Pupil stabiliser30 mm after the F/20.5 focus. 1° tilt: PSF shift 1.05 mm (5.8″ on sky), pupil shift 73% of diameter. Max needed ≈ 0.33° for 25% pupil drift (FoV drift ≈ 1.9″).
Fast tip-tilt130 mm upstream of the DM. 1″ on sky = 0.008° rotation = 0.11% pupil shift on the DM.
DM468Pupil 31.6 mm, 12° AOI → 31.6 × 32.3 mm on a 33 mm clear aperture. Measured flat ≈ 6 nm RMS, TT stroke ≈ 15 µm surface.
DichroicLong-pass, AOI 15°. Custom part with 0.1° wedge on the second face to reduce astigmatism and NCPA; quotations pending.
StatusAligned in Padova (June 2026), transmitted WFE ≈ 55 nm RMS over 33 mm.

WFS Channel

BS1 sends about 30% of the visible light to the VIS port. A re-imaging lens puts the pupil on the modulator and makes an F/45 focus. BS2 sends about 10% to the Gain Scheduling Camera (GSC) and 90% to the pyramid. A fold mirror before the pyramid focus can feed visiting WFSs.

Filters in front of the camera

We usually place a narrowband filter or a neutral density (ND) filter in front of the ORCA. A filter of thickness t and index n in the converging beam moves the focus back by about t(n − 1)/n (roughly a third of its thickness for glass). The ORCA is aligned so that it takes this defocus into account: it's in focus with a filter in place. Removing the filter, or using one of a different thickness, needs a refocus.

Don't let strong light reach the sensor without an ND filter: the manual warns that too much light, such as a high-energy laser, can damage the CMOS sensor.

Filtri davanti alla camera

Di solito mettiamo un filtro a banda stretta o un filtro a densità neutra (ND) davanti alla ORCA. Un filtro di spessore t e indice n nel fascio convergente sposta indietro il fuoco di circa t(n − 1)/n (circa un terzo del suo spessore per il vetro). La ORCA è allineata tenendo conto di questo defocus: è a fuoco con un filtro montato. Togliere il filtro, o usarne uno di spessore diverso, richiede di rifocheggiare.

Non far arrivare luce intensa sul sensore senza un filtro ND: il manuale avverte che troppa luce, per esempio un laser ad alta energia, può danneggiare il sensore CMOS.

Filtres devant la caméra

Nous plaçons généralement un filtre à bande étroite ou un filtre de densité neutre (ND) devant l’ORCA. Un filtre d’épaisseur t et d’indice n dans le faisceau convergent recule le foyer d’environ t(n − 1)/n (environ un tiers de son épaisseur pour du verre). L’ORCA est alignée en tenant compte de cette défocalisation : elle est au point avec un filtre en place. Retirer le filtre, ou en utiliser un d’une autre épaisseur, demande de refaire la mise au point.

Ne laissez pas une lumière intense atteindre le capteur sans filtre ND : le manuel avertit qu’un excès de lumière, par exemple un laser de forte énergie, peut endommager le capteur CMOS.
