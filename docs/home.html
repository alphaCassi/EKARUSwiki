Highlights

News, recent results from observations, and anything the whole team should know.

  • 31 August – 1 September 2026 Official first light of EKARUS at the Copernico telescope.Done
  • June 2026 Common path aligned in Padova. Transmitted wavefront error about 55 nm RMS over the 33 mm DM pupil, mostly coma and secondary astigmatism, to be corrected by the DM. Fast tip-tilt mirror tested and integrated.
  • Spring 2026 DM468 characterized at Arcetri. Best flat about 6 nm RMS wavefront, small actuator print-through, tip-tilt stroke about 15 µm surface.
See more
  • April 2026 Coudé relay upgraded. New L1 and L3 lenses installed to correct the chromatic focus of the old relay. First seeing-limited PSFs (about 1.8″ seeing) show no strong aberrations from the new relay.
  • April 2026 PAPYRUS decommissioned after reaching almost 65% Strehl at 1450 nm on the T152. Its components were shipped from LAM to Asiago for EKARUS.

Source: EKARUS Newsletter.
